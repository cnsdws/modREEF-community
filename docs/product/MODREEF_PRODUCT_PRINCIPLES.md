#  # modREEF Product Principles

**Version:** 1.0

## Core Philosophy

**The software should do the work.**

Technology should disappear into the background so the aquarist can focus on the reef, not the controller.

---

## 1. Technology Should Disappear

Users should think about fish, corals, and reef health—not IP addresses, outlet numbers, drivers, cloud accounts, or protocols.

---

## 2. Infer Before Asking

Always infer before asking.

- Detect devices automatically.
- Identify capabilities automatically.
- Guess equipment using behavior and power usage.
- Ask only for confirmation.

---

## 3. Discovery Before Configuration

Users add equipment.

modREEF discovers devices.

Configuration should be the exception, not the rule.

---

## 4. Invisible Complexity

Power belongs in the software.

The UI should remain simple regardless of system complexity.

---

## 5. Three Decision Rule

No common task should require more than three user decisions.

---

## 6. Progressive Disclosure

Beginner users see simple controls.

Advanced users can reveal more.

Experts can access everything.

---

## 7. Explain Recommendations

Every recommendation includes an explanation.

Never be a black box.

---

## 8. Local First

Critical automation runs locally.

Cloud is optional.

---

## 9. Hardware Agnostic

Support capabilities, not brands.

Users choose equipment.

modREEF adapts.

---

## 10. Consumer Experience

The benchmark is Chromecast, AirPods, and Sonos.

Discovery should feel effortless.

---

## 11. Confidence-Based Decisions

Show confidence and explain why.

Example:

> Return Pump (96% confidence)

Explain the reasoning.

---

## 12. Safety First

Never automate high-risk actions without approval.

Protect livestock before convenience.

---

## 13. Open Platform

Support many vendors.

Encourage community drivers and device profiles.

---

# Engineering Rule

**Every question the software asks is a design failure unless the information cannot be safely inferred or discovered.**

---

# Final Test

Before implementing any feature, ask:

> **Does this make the aquarist's life simpler?**

If not, redesign it.
